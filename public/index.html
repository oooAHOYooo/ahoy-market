Store Support & FAQ

Sovereign media, physical pressing fulfillment, and order help
24/7 Dispatch

What does "Sovereign Ownership" mean?

Unlike traditional subscription streaming services where your access expires when you stop paying, purchases on AHOY Market inscribe permanent ownership to your Sovereign AHOY ID. You can stream forever on AHOY Player, download raw lossless WAV/MP3 files, or transfer the entitlement directly to another listener.

How does physical shipping work?

All physical products (Hand-Burned CDs and Archival NFC Cards) are crafted in-house. Physical orders are processed within 24 to 48 hours and shipped via USPS First-Class with tracking. We ship domestically and internationally.

Can I send a physical edition as a gift?

Yes. In the Production Terminal on the right, select "Gift to Friend" to specify their shipping address, custom sleeve dedication (printed directly on the sleeve), and their AHOY ID so they receive both physical delivery and digital library access.

Artist Revenue & Platform Fees

AHOY operates on a 0% platform fee model for direct artist tips and boosts. 100% of your contributions go straight to the artist's payout balance.

Need help with an order or entitlement?

Reach our operations team directly at support@ahoy.ooo or open an inquiry on the AHOY Network node. Please include your AHOY ID or purchase transaction ID.
