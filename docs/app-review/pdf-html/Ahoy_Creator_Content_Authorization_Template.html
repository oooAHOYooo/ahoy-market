The creator/rights holder confirms that they own or control the rights necessary to submit the listed content to Ahoy Indie Media and authorize Little Market LLC / Ahoy Indie Media to host, stream, display, promote, catalog, and distribute that content through Ahoy-controlled products and services.

This authorization includes:

  • Music videos.
  • Films, shorts, live performances, clips, interviews, and show episodes.
  • Podcast audio and podcast metadata.
  • Artwork, thumbnails, photos, logos, titles, descriptions, tags, and other catalog/discovery metadata.
  • Scheduled or channel-style presentation inside Ahoy, including the Ahoy AHOY TV marketing surface.
  • Mobile, web, desktop, CarPlay, Android Auto, and other Ahoy-controlled app experiences.
  • App-store review, screenshots, previews, caching, hosting, CDN delivery, search, discovery, and promotional display related to operating Ahoy.

  • They have authority to grant this permission.
