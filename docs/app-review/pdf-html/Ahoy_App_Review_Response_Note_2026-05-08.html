We have attached documentation showing Ahoy’s authorization to stream and display the music, podcast, video, catalog, and discovery content available in the app.

Ahoy is a direct-to-artist media platform. The listed creators and rights holders granted Ahoy permission to display their artist profiles, catalog metadata, audio, video, podcast, and related discovery content in the Ahoy iOS app. The attached artist rights schedule identifies each platform artist and cross-references the supporting authorization records.

For the AHOY TV surface, Ahoy is not providing access to third-party TV providers, cable channels, IPTV services, or external broadcast streams. AHOY TV is a marketing-style scheduled viewing surface for Ahoy-owned, Ahoy-produced, or Ahoy-authorized platform video content. The attached register identifies the creators/rights holders and the supporting authorization records.

Thank you.

Important Caveat

If everyone on the platform gave rights through onboarding or a creator agreement, attach that agreement too. Apple usually wants the actual rights grant, not just the app’s internal artist list.

The safest package is:

  1. A signed company statement from Little Market LLC / Ahoy.
  2. The artist schedule CSV or a PDF version of it.
  3. The creator terms/agreement showing the rights grant.
  4. Any direct email approvals or contracts for artists not covered by the standard terms.
  5. A clear AHOY TV explanation stating that it is an Ahoy-programmed marketing/discovery surface for owned or authorized catalog videos, not a third-party television stream aggregator.
