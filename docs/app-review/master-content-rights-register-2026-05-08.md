## Platform Rights Position

Ahoy Indie Media is a direct-to-artist media platform. The content available in the app is either:

1. Produced by Ahoy / Little Market LLC.
2. Submitted by creators, artists, hosts, or rights holders who authorized Ahoy to display, stream, promote, and catalog their content.
3. Licensed or otherwise cleared by Ahoy for app distribution.

Ahoy's "AHOY TV" feature is not a third-party cable, broadcast, IPTV, or unauthorized channel aggregation service. It is a marketing-style scheduled viewing surface that presents Ahoy-owned, Ahoy-produced, or Ahoy-authorized video programming from the platform catalog. In the codebase, AHOY TV is generated from `content_shows` and grouped into editorial channels such as Misc, Films, Music Videos, and Live Shows.

## Rights Scope To Document

For each creator or rights holder, keep evidence that covers:

- Artist/profile display.
- Catalog metadata, including names, titles, artwork, thumbnails, tags, descriptions, and discovery/search indexing.
- Audio streaming.
- Video streaming.
- Podcast streaming.
- Scheduled or channel-style presentation, including the Ahoy AHOY TV marketing surface.
- Mobile app distribution, including iOS, Android, web, desktop, CarPlay, Android Auto, and other Ahoy-controlled clients.
- Use of third-party infrastructure required to operate the service, including Apple, Google, hosting/CDN, payment, analytics, and app-store review systems.

## Suggested Company Statement

Little Market LLC / Ahoy Indie Media confirms that the Ahoy app does not provide unauthorized access to third-party streaming services, third-party TV providers, cable channels, or external discovery catalogs. The app presents content produced by Ahoy/Little Market LLC or submitted by artists, creators, hosts, and rights holders who authorized Ahoy to stream, display, promote, and catalog their content in the Ahoy app.

The AHOY TV section is a scheduled marketing and discovery surface for Ahoy-controlled programming. It groups existing Ahoy video catalog items into channels such as Films, Music Videos, Live Shows, and Misc. It is not a retransmission of third-party television channels.

## Maintenance Rule

No content should be published to Ahoy unless the rights evidence exists first. If rights evidence is missing, the content should stay unpublished, hidden, or internal until the creator/rights holder authorization is complete.
