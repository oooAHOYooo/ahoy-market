# Unboxing Order & Assembly

How to pack each Ahoy Console box for the best customer experience.

## Packing sequence (per unit, ~2 minutes)

### Step 1: Prepare components
- [ ] Pi Zero 2W (freshly flashed SD card already installed)
- [ ] 1m mini HDMI → HDMI cable
- [ ] Micro USB OTG adapter
- [ ] Printed quick-start card
- [ ] Small tissue/kraft paper for padding

### Step 2: Wrap Pi
- Gently wrap Pi in tissue paper to prevent scratches
- Don't wrap too tight (micro HDMI port should be accessible)
- Place centered in box

### Step 3: Arrange cables
- Coil mini HDMI cable loosely (~4" loops), place on one side of Pi
- Place OTG adapter in small kraft envelope (label: "USB adapter"), place on other side
- Use tissue paper to fill gaps and prevent shifting

### Step 4: Add quick-start card
- Place on **top** of everything (first thing customer sees)
- Angle slightly so text is visible when box is opened

### Step 5: Close and seal
- Close box flaps smoothly
- Apply Ahoy logo sticker/seal on center seam (optional, for tamper-evident feel)
- Stack in staging area

## Quality checklist (before sealing)

- [ ] SD card installed (verify by opening case briefly if not visible)
- [ ] No bent pins on Pi
- [ ] HDMI cable has no kinks or damage
- [ ] OTG adapter not loose/damaged
- [ ] Quick-start card is readable (clear print)
- [ ] Box flaps close smoothly without forcing
- [ ] No items shift when shaking gently

## Storage

- **Before packaging:** Keep components in climate-controlled area (avoid humidity that degrades cardboard)
- **After packaging:** Stack boxes flat (max 5 boxes high) to prevent crushing
- **Transport:** Use sturdy boxes within boxes for market transport (to protect outer branding)

## Batch handling

For 20-unit assembly day:
- **Setup time:** ~20 minutes (layout components, test 1 unit end-to-end)
- **Assembly time:** ~40 minutes (20 units × 2 min/unit)
- **QA time:** ~15 minutes (spot-check every 5th unit)
- **Total:** ~1.5 hours for 20 units

Hire help or break into two 30-minute sessions with a friend.

## For remote add-ons (sold separately)

If customer buys a remote at the farmers market:

**Option A: Packed together**
- Include remote + receiver dongle in the box with console
- (Only if bundling, not for separate sales)

**Option B: Separate sleeve**
- Remote goes in its own small kraft sleeve
- Label: "Ahoy Remote — USB WiFi"
- Pair with a quick-start sticker: "Plug into white adapter on your console"

## Notes for future scale

- Consider a **bi-fold insert card** (printed both sides, folds in half) instead of a loose card—more professional
- Add **seed paper** or **plantable element** to the box (Ahoy plants a tree per console sold? Nice story angle)
- Use **compostable fill material** instead of tissue (better for premium positioning)
