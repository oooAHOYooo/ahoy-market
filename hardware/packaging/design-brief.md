# Cardboard Packaging Design Brief

## Box specifications

### Dimensions (outside)
- **Width:** 7 inches (177mm)
- **Height:** 5 inches (127mm)
- **Depth:** 3.5 inches (89mm)

Fits Pi Zero 2W (2.9" × 1.3" × 0.5") with room for cables and inserts.

### Material
- **Stock:** 2-ply kraft cardboard (natural tan/brown color preferred, feels premium)
- **Finish:** Matte (not glossy—avoids cheap plastic feel)
- **Print:** 2-color (Ahoy brand color + 1 accent, e.g., white/pink or white/purple)

### Printing spec

**Front panel (7" × 5"):**
- **Ahoy logo** (center top, ~2.5" wide)
- **Tagline:** "Plug in. Connect. Stream." (minimal, centered)
- **Color:** White text + Ahoy pink (`#ff0060`) accent bar at bottom

**Back panel (7" × 5"):**
- **What's inside:** Icon grid showing Pi, HDMI cable, OTG adapter
- **Key features:** "Works with any TV" + "Simple WiFi setup" + "Independent media"
- **Contact:** QR code to `setup.ahoy.ooo` (or your help page) + email address

**Side panels (3.5" × 5"):**
- **Left:** "Ahoy" logo repeated, minimal
- **Right:** "Made with ♥ for independent creators" + version/date (e.g., "v1 · May 2026")

**Bottom (7" × 3.5"):**
- **Requirements:** "5V USB power" + "WiFi required" + "HDMI-compatible TV"
- **Small text:** Raspberry Pi compatibility, no warranty text (keep it light)

## Color palette

| Element | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Logo/brand text | `#ff0060` (Ahoy pink) | Main accent |
| Secondary text | `#7c3aed` (Purple) | Optional accent |
| Backgrounds | White or kraft (natural) | Majority of box |
| Warning/alerts | Dark gray | Rare (keep minimal) |

## Design notes

- **Aesthetic:** Minimal, clean, friendly (not tech-heavy)
- **Target vibe:** "This is intentionally simple hardware" (like Sonos early days)
- **Avoid:** Glossy graphics, many colors, technical jargon
- **Tone:** Fun but professional (e.g., "Plug in. Connect. Stream." not "Turn your TV into a media center")

### Recommended: Packhelp or local print shop
- **Local:** Fast turnaround, can tweak design in person, slightly cheaper
- **Packhelp:** No design experience needed, templates, good results, international shipping

## File format & handoff

**Preferred format for printer:** PDF, 300 DPI (for print), with bleed (0.125" on all sides)

**Design tools:**
- Use Canva (free, simple) or Adobe InDesign
- Export as PDF for print shop
- Provide dimensions exactly as above (account for bleed)
